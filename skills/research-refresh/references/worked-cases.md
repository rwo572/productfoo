# Worked refresh cases

These are fictional instruction examples, not observed customer evidence. Use
them to reason about the decision consequence; do not infer universal thresholds
or actual skill effectiveness from them.

## A changed constraint warrants an amendment

Decision: choose a supplier whose approved deployment region includes Region A.
Existing claim: supplier supports Region A; the supplied baseline reference
records that support. New approved evidence: the supplied current reference
states that new deployments there are unavailable.

Propose replacing the availability claim and reopening the affected shortlist
decision. Cite both supplied references and dates. Do not rewrite unrelated
capability sections or automatically change the decision itself.

## Routine news yields a scoped no-op

Decision: choose a supplier by deployment region and audit requirements. New
approved evidence: a launch announcement about a cosmetic interface update,
with no change to either criterion.

Return no material updates in the checked scope, including the window and
announcement reference. Preserve the original analysis. Do not force a new
recommendation merely to make the refresh look productive.

## Contradiction remains visible without an immediate decision change

Decision: shortlist suppliers; deployment coverage is still awaiting verification.
Two current approved references disagree about Region A availability.

Record both claims and their dates as contradictory evidence. Propose a lower
confidence statement or explicit verification question; do not choose the
convenient source or omit the conflict because the shortlist has not changed.

## Missing required coverage cannot prove no change

Decision: reassess a shortlist after a required compliance check. That required
source is unavailable or its paper fails validation.

Report the incomplete comparison and the next action. Do not report a no-op.
An unavailable optional source may instead be listed as omitted, with any
conclusion restricted to the evidence actually checked.
