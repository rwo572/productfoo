# Staged Opportunity Assessment (0→1 · 1→n · n^x)

This is the opportunity-assessment spine `foo pitch` fuses into the FAQ. It is a
**staged** framework: the questions change with the *kind* of bet, because a net-new
product, a major enhancement, and an incremental improvement need different scrutiny.

> **How to use it (the operating rules — carry these into the Pitch paper):**
> - The goal is **not to have all the answers** — it is to give leaders the fodder to
>   assess the opportunity. **Be concise. Keep it to ~2 pages and hit the highlights.**
> - This is an **early read** for executives, founders, and other leaders on the
>   opportunity of what we are building. The detailed build spec comes later, not here.
> - **Bring data where you have it; caveat where you don't** but are looking into it.
> - **Link out** (docs, dashboards, reports) when that is the easiest way to make the point.
> - Keep the reader-facing answers in plain prose with ordinary Markdown citations.
>   Type each load-bearing claim in the same document's **Evidence ledger** using
>   `Claim | Source type | Confidence | Citation`. A back-of-the-napkin estimate is
>   fine when its source type is `hypothesis` and the ledger says what would validate it.

Pick the stage first (the command auto-suggests from the request; confirm with the user):

- **0 → 1** — a brand-new product introducing a unique solution / creating a new market.
  Transition from *no product* to the first version. Highest innovation + market-introduction risk.
- **1 → n** — significant enhancement or expansion of an existing product: new features,
  new markets, or a materially stronger value proposition for evolving demand.
- **n^x** — incremental improvements to an established product: refined UX, efficiency,
  smaller features that *cumulatively* move performance and satisfaction.

## For a New Product (0 → 1)

**Defining the Customer and Their Needs**
- Who is our specific target customer?
- What are the precise problems or unmet needs for this customer group?
- How are these customers currently solving these problems?
- What makes these problems worth solving *now*?

**Market Understanding and Scope**
- How large is the potential market (TAM, SAM, SOM)? Will these be net-new customers, or will we sell into / drive adoption with existing customers? (back-of-the-napkin is just fine)
- What are the growth trends and projections for this market?
- How might external factors (economic, social, technological) affect market size and demand?

**Product and Competitive Landscape**
- What existing user expectations will we have to consider?
- What existing solutions are customers currently using?
- What are the limitations or gaps in these existing solutions?
- How will our product address these gaps uniquely?
- How might competitors respond to our entry into the market?

**Product Differentiation and Risk Analysis**
- What is the story we want to tell in market when this launches (a couple of sentences or a handful of bullets)? How does this storyline ladder up to the larger storylines we are telling in market?
- What are our product's unique selling points (USPs)?
- What are the key risks and challenges of this product's development and market entry?
- How might changes in technology, customer preferences, or market conditions impact our product's relevance and usability?

**Market Entry Strategy**
- What is our entry strategy into the market?
- How do we plan to reach our initial customers?
- If we find initial success, what would we want to do to scale up?
- What partnerships or collaborations might be beneficial?

**Measuring Success**
- What are the key metrics for measuring the success of this product?
- What are our revenue projections and profitability forecasts?
- How will we manage and mitigate the risks associated with these projections?
- What early indicators can we monitor to gauge potential success or needed adjustments before broader metrics can be assessed?
- How will we ensure the success metrics demonstrate the *incremental* value of the new product rather than shifts from existing solutions?
- What guardrail metrics will we monitor so the pursuit of success doesn't negatively impact other important aspects (e.g., customer satisfaction, brand perception)?

**Investment**
- What skills and experience are critical to the success of the project?
- What is the first milestone to evaluate further investment?

## For a Major Enhancement (1 → n)

**Understanding the Customer Evolution**
- How have our customers' needs and expectations evolved since the original product launch?
- What feedback or data points highlight the need for this major enhancement?

**Enhancement Impact and Scope**
- How will this enhancement improve or change the current customer experience?
- What are the projected impacts on customer retention, acquisition, and overall market share?
- How does the product interoperate with existing systems and patterns impacting the end-user experience?
- What are the risks of implementing these enhancements, including potential customer backlash or technical challenges?
- How do we prioritize which improvements to implement based on customer impact, feasibility, and cost?

**Market and Competitive Reaction**
- What is the story we want to tell in market when this launches? How does this storyline ladder up to the larger storylines we are telling in market?
- How might this enhancement alter our position within the competitive landscape?
- How are competitors likely to respond to our product enhancement?

**Operational and Financial Implications**
- What changes to our operations, sales enablement, etc. are necessary to roll out this enhancement?
- How will this enhancement affect our cost structure and profitability?

**Measurement of Success**
- What specific metrics will we use to evaluate the success of this enhancement?
- How will we track the impact of this enhancement on overall product performance and customer satisfaction?
- What immediate changes in user behavior or feedback can we use as early signs of impact?
- How will we differentiate growth/benefits attributed to the enhancement from general market trends or other variables?
- While striving for enhancement success, what factors must remain stable or improved to avoid undermining existing product value?

## For Incremental Improvements (n^x)

**Customer Centricity**
- What minor customer pain points are we addressing with these incremental improvements?
- How isolated is the customer pain, and is there a larger trend that requires additional investigation?
- How do these improvements align with our customers' evolving needs and expectations?

**Scope and Impact Analysis**
- What is the scope of these incremental improvements?
- How do we prioritize which improvements to implement based on customer impact, feasibility, and cost?

**Competitive Considerations and Risks**
- What is the story we want to tell in market when this launches? How does this storyline ladder up to the larger storylines we are telling in market?
- Will these improvements help maintain or enhance our competitive edge?
- Where do we expect to be copied?
- Is this introducing a strong or weak moat?
- What risks do these improvements pose to our existing product functionality and customer base?

**Operational Execution and Scalability**
- How will these improvements be implemented within the current product framework?
- What operational, technical, or logistical challenges are anticipated, and how will they be addressed?
- What is the roll-out strategy?
- Where do you anticipate change to users' workflows leading to excitement, fear, doubt, etc., and how can we communicate this in advance?

**Success Metrics and Feedback Loops**
- What metrics will be used to assess the success of these incremental improvements?
- How will customer feedback be integrated into future improvement cycles?
- What small-scale feedback or analytics can serve as quick checks for effectiveness?
- In what ways can we measure the specific contribution of each incremental change to understand its value-add clearly?
- What are the non-negotiable performance metrics that must not be degraded by these improvements?
