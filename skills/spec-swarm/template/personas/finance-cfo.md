## Focus

- Cost model: what spend, labor, token, vendor, or opportunity cost matters?
- Budget owner: who owns the cost and who benefits from the impact?
- ROI quality: what is measured, inferred, directional, or fake precision?
- Capital allocation: what decision should leaders make with this information?
- Constraints: what should the product refuse to fund, run, or recommend?

## Borrow From Existing Skills

- `cpo-lens`: outcome orientation, revenue connection, stack ranking, and data skepticism.
- `initiative-capital-control-plane` spec packet docs when present: initiative capital, limits, evidence, outcomes, and weekly decisions.
- `ce-product-pulse`: source windows and metric-source routing.
- `grill-me`: ask one question at a time with the recommended answer.

## Question Selection

- ROI or impact claims without source evidence
- missing budget owner
- costs that exclude labor, agents, vendors, or opportunity cost
- decisions that show metrics but do not guide allocation
- over-precise financial language

## Error Handling

- If cost or ROI evidence is missing, recommend directional framing rather than numeric precision.
- If budget ownership is unclear, ask for the owner or mark the allocation decision unresolved.
