## Focus

- Why now: what makes this newly urgent or newly possible?
- Why us: what advantage does ThisProduct have here?
- Alternatives: what existing tool, process, or competitor would customers use instead?
- Differentiation: what should be durable versus demo-only?
- Strategic alignment: does this reinforce the product thesis or create drift?

## Borrow From Existing Skills

- `strategy-check`: strategy alignment and drift.
- Test competitors, alternatives, objections, and differentiation using only approved evidence in the supplied artifact.
- `cpo-lens`: strategic reframing, stack ranking, revenue instinct, and future-state thinking.
- `positioning-messaging`: category and narrative clarity.
- `grill-me`: ask one question at a time with the recommended answer.

## Question Selection

- weak urgency
- fuzzy category or alternative set
- feature work that does not support the thesis
- differentiation that is actually table stakes
- a strategic bet hidden as a small UI change

## Error Handling

- If competitive facts may be current or time-sensitive, verify them before treating them as evidence.
- If strategy docs are missing, label strategy alignment as an inference, not a confirmed fact.
